About Tester.io

Quality engineering, refined.

We founded Tester.io in 2018 because we were tired of QA being treated like an afterthought — the rushed handoff between engineering and release. We rebuilt the discipline from first principles: automated where it matters, manual where it counts, and observable everywhere.

Today we work with founder-led startups and Fortune 500 platform teams who share one belief — that how a product feels in production is a feature, not an accident. Every test we author is a small promise to your user.
