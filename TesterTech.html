Field Note Nº 03

Why Tester Tech.

For twelve years we built tools that broke other people's software before their users did. Test harnesses, performance grids, audit pipelines. Somewhere along the way we started asking the obvious question: what would it look like if hardware was held to the same standard?

Tester Tech is the answer. Each device passes through the same drop rigs, climate chambers, and synthetic-load loops we use for our QA work — only now it's our own silicon, our own firmware, our own promise on the line.

01

Engineering rigor

500-hour soak tests. Drop rigs that mimic two years of pocket life in a week. Every firmware build runs against the same automated suite as our enterprise software.

02

Materials honesty

Grade-5 titanium. Sapphire crystal. Recycled aluminium. Bio-acetate frames. We publish our bill of materials and our repair scores. The product is the proof, not the page.

03

Tested in public

Every device ships with a public test report — battery curves, water-resistance results, radio-emission logs. If we say a watch lasts seven days, the chart is on the product page.
