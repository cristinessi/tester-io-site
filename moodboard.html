Considered, not chosen

The other directions.

Two credible alternatives I worked through before committing.

Direction A

Quiet Expertise

Senior, restrained, monochromatic.

Stripped-back, all monochromatic restraint. Reads more like a senior consultant's bio than a builder's portfolio. Rejected for being too quiet — the work I'm doing now is exploratory, and the design needed to signal warmth and play.

Direction B

Warm Minimalism

Light, soft, breathy.

Cream backgrounds, soft serifs, lots of whitespace. The “thoughtful indie” aesthetic. Rejected because the technical work — backends, deployment, AI integration — needed a more committed surface. Cream feels journal-y; the projects deserve gravity.

When you see it

Across every page on this site — the homepage, the about, the trend finder, the sights — the same five things repeat: gold against ink, italic Fraunces for the warm moments, JetBrains Mono for the technical ones, hairline borders for structure, and restraint as a discipline. That's the system. This is the moodboard.
